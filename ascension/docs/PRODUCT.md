## What ASCENSION is

A **Personal Transformation Operating System** for one person, Russ, with one
purpose: daily, intentional execution toward the Prime version of himself.

It is explicitly **not** a habit tracker, productivity app, to-do list,
dashboard, or project management tool. The difference is stance: those tools
ask the user to operate them; ASCENSION operates the day and asks Russ only
to live it.

## Operating principles (as built)

1. **Reduce thinking.** Every screen answers "what now?" before it is asked.
   The Daily screen reorders itself by time of day; the outfit and fragrance
   arrive pre-decided; recommendations never change mid-day.
2. **One number.** Prime status (0–100, with its state word) is the only
   verdict the app renders. Everything else is a checklist or a quiet chart.
3. **Rituals, not tasks.** The day opens with the Morning Protocol and closes
   with a seal — three reflection prompts and a frozen score. Sealing is the
   product's emotional center: the day ends *on purpose*.
4. **Standards over goals.** The Standards card is a daily re-commitment to
   identity-level behaviors (posture, speech, presence), not a task list.
5. **Premium calm.** Near-black, one champagne accent, serif display type,
   hairlines, no badges, no confetti, no red. The app never nags, never
   gamifies, never celebrates loudly. The reward is the number and the streak.
6. **His words, his system.** Everything the app says is short, direct,
   second person. Everything it tracks is editable in System — the defaults
   are a strong starting opinion, not a cage.

## The Daily contract

Morning: wordmark → greeting → Prime ring → Morning Protocol (with the
day's intention) → Appearance (outfit + fragrance, already chosen) →
Standards → Hydration.

Evening/night: Night Protocol (ending in **Close the day**) rises to the
top; the sealed banner with the frozen score replaces it after the ritual.

Daily must remain the screen Russ *wants* to open at 05:30 and at 22:00. Any
feature that makes it noisier does not ship.
